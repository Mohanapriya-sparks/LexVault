# LexVault — Cryptographic References & Research Foundations

LexVault builds upon established academic literature in zero-knowledge cryptography, algebraic hash functions, threshold access control, and electronic evidence standards.

---

## 1. Zero-Knowledge Proofs & Circuits

1. **Groth, J. (2016).** *"On the Size of Pairing-based Non-interactive Arguments."*
   - *EUROCRYPT 2016.* [Cryptology ePrint 2016/260](https://eprint.iacr.org/2016/260).
   - *Application in LexVault:* Underpins the constant-size (3 group elements $A \in \mathbb{G}_1, B \in \mathbb{G}_2, C \in \mathbb{G}_1$) non-interactive zero-knowledge proof generated by SnarkJS and verified by `Groth16Verifier.sol`.

2. **Iden3 / Circom Team (2021).** *"Circom 2.0: A Scalable Circuit Compiler for Zero-Knowledge Proofs."*
   - [iden3/circom](https://github.com/iden3/circom).
   - *Application in LexVault:* Circuit DSL defining rank-1 constraint systems (R1CS) for Merkle leaf membership and commitment validation (`evidence_verifier.circom`).

---

## 2. Algebraic Hashing & Polynomial Secret Sharing

3. **Grassi, L., Lüftenegger, R., Rechberger, C., Rotaru, D., & Schofnegger, M. (2021).** *"Poseidon: A New Hash Function for Zero-Knowledge Proof Systems."*
   - *USENIX Security 2021.* [Cryptology ePrint 2019/458](https://eprint.iacr.org/2019/458).
   - *Application in LexVault:* Used for internal binary Merkle tree node compression over the BN128 scalar field, achieving orders of magnitude fewer R1CS constraints compared to SHA-256 in Circom.

4. **Shamir, A. (1979).** *"How to Share a Secret."*
   - *Communications of the ACM, 22(11), 612–613.* [DOI: 10.1145/359168.359176](https://doi.org/10.1145/359168.359176).
   - *Application in LexVault:* 2-of-3 threshold access scheme implemented over the prime field $\mathbb{F}_r$ using polynomial degree 1 and Lagrange interpolation at $x=0$.

---

## 3. Electronic Evidence & Chain of Custody Standards

5. **NIST Special Publication (SP) 800-86.** *"Guide to Integrating Forensic Techniques into Incident Response."*
   - National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST).
   - *Application in LexVault:* Informs the sequential custody log schema, cryptographically linking time, custodian identity, and transfer intent.

6. **ISO/IEC 27037:2012.** *"Information technology — Security techniques — Guidelines for identification, collection, acquisition and preservation of digital evidence."*
   - International Organization for Standardization.
   - *Application in LexVault:* Guides the post-registration integrity invariant and tamper-evident audit record formatting.
